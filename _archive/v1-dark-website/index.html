01 · About

Defender first, with attacker fluency.

I'm a cybersecurity analyst focused on the day-to-day work of a SOC: triaging alerts, hunting threats, validating findings, and turning investigations into actionable intelligence. My most representative project — 33 threat investigations on the Burien municipal network — mirrors L1/L2 analyst work using Elastic SIEM and Suricata.

I also hold the Mile2 C)PTE (Certified Penetration Testing Engineer) alongside CompTIA Security+. The combination matters: defenders who have walked an attacker's path build sharper detection rules and triage faster.

Looking to bring this mindset to a SOC team that values careful documentation, honest investigation methodology, and continuous detection-engineering improvement.
